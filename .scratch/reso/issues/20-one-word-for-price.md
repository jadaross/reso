# 20 — One word for price: £ ££ £££ everywhere

**What's wrong:** The same Price Tier is spelled five ways on four screens: "Medium month" in the
header, "MEDIUM" on the ticket stamp, "Low · Medium · High" on the Places counts and on Past,
"£ Cheap · ££ Middling · £££ Expensive" on the add form and the Month Vote, and "The rota says cheap"
under the vote. Every persona in the November audit paused on at least one; Scotty worked out that
Middling = Medium = ££ only while writing his report. The £ signs were the one version nobody had to
decode.

**What to build:** One vocabulary for Price Tier on every screen: £, ££, £££, with the pound signs
doing the work. A single plain word may sit beside them as a secondary label if it helps, but it is
the same word everywhere.

**Blocked by:** nothing

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Dinner header reads "££ month", the ticket stamp reads "££", and Past reads "££" for the tier
- [ ] The Places counts, the add form's tier picker and the Month Vote all use £ / ££ / £££ as the primary label
- [ ] The vote's rota line reads "rota says £" (or ££, £££)
- [ ] The Admin tier override buttons use the same £ glyphs
- [ ] One helper formats a Price Tier for display, and every screen calls it; no screen spells a tier by hand
- [ ] "Dinner party" keeps its name; it is a kind of Outing, not a Price Tier
- [ ] The words Low/Medium/High and Cheap/Middling/Expensive no longer appear as the sole label anywhere on screen
